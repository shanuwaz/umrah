Makkah's Emaar Al Manar Hotel **** is only 850 meters from the Great Mosque, 4.8 km from Makkah Museum, and 10 km from both Hira Cave and Assalamu Alaika Ayyuha Annabi. It's perfect for all group sizes arriving for Umrah, featuring spacious family rooms and standard suites with quality beds, a kettle, a private bathroom with free toiletries and shower, a fridge, complimentary Wi-Fi, a flatscreen TV, and safety deposit boxes. A coffee house and snack bar onsite offers delicious refreshments and beverages. It has soundproof suites, facilities for disabled guests, a lift, concierge services, luggage storage, in-room tea and coffee facilities, laundry and dry cleaning, ironing facilities, and multilingual staff.
