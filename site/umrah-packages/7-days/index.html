Documents Required for Umrah from India

Before booking a suitable Umrah package from India, pilgrims must prepare an important set of necessary paperwork. Missing even one of these can lead to Umrah visa rejections, so plan carefully.

● Indian Passport with Validity: Ensure your passport is valid for a period of at least 6 months from the date of travel for Umrah. It must also possess a minimum of 2 blank pages for stamps.

● Passport Scan: Get scanned copies of the first and last page of your Indian passport, with no blurs.

● 2 Color Photos: Provide 2 clear passport-sized color photographs, taken not over 3 to 6 months back against a plain white background. The photos must clearly show your facial features.

● PAN Card: Give a copy of your government-issued PAN Card as valid ID proof, useful for Umrah trip booking confirmation and not the travel itself.

● Hotel Booking Confirmation: Submit evidence of authentic accommodation bookings in both Makkah and Madinah hotels, with Nusuk approval and specific BRN (Booking Reference Number).

● Flight Details: It is mandatory to provide your full flight itinerary, with details of round-trip air tickets for your Umrah pilgrimage. This proves your intent to leave Saudi after the pilgrimage.

● Umrah Visa: A tourist visa may suffice in some cases to perform Umrah from India, but it is highly recommended to obtain a specific Umrah visa for a smoother travel experience.

● Vaccination Certificates: All adults and children aged over 1 must submit proof of vaccination against Meningococcal Quadrivalent (ACWY). Some airlines and Umrah groups may also ask for COVID vaccination. Pilgrims do not require Meningitis or Yellow Fever vaccinations unless transiting through specific routes/countries.

● Written Consent for Minors: A written consent for travel must be provided by the parents or acceptable legal guardians of children under 18 years old to go to Makkah for Umrah. They must also bring their birth certificates along.

● Additional Documents: Women under 45 traveling alone for Umrah must carry an NOC (No Objection Certificate) from their Mahram. For converts, it is essential to submit a letter from a mosque or equivalent Islamic center confirming their Muslim faith and identity. Those staying with friends or relatives in Saudi during their Umrah trip need to give an Iqama copy of the host.

The rules for Umrah travel, essential documentation list, Umrah visa fees, and other applicable policies may change from time to time as per the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah in Saudi Arabia.
