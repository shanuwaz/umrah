      Best Places to Visit During Umrah Trip

      There are several holy sites to tour when booking Cheap Umrah 2026 packages for your pilgrimage, regardless of the time of year you visit. A few of the must-see ones include:

      • Masjid al-Haram: An absolute must-see when booking budget Umrah tour packages, Masjid al-Haram is without a question the holiest mosque in the holy city of Makkah (or Mecca). This is where the pilgrims will perform the Tawaf round the Kaaba, moving on to then taking a drink from the sacred Zamzam well.
      • Jabal al-Nour: Otherwise called the Mountain of Light, this spectacular mountain is famously home to a cave called Gar-e-Hira. According to Islamic history, this is where the Quran was first revealed to the blessed Prophet Muhammed.
      • Masjid-e-Ayesha: In addition to being the second-largest mosque in the holy city of Makkah, this beautiful mosque is also one of the oldest structures in the city.
      • Mount Arafat: This is a must-see site when booking low-cost Umrah packages 2026 from India, as it is believed to be the place where the blessed Prophet Muhammed imparted his last discourse.
      • Masjid an-Nabawi: It is a very holy site that houses the tomb of the blessed Prophet Muhammed. The famous mosque is also highly acclaimed for its magnificent architecture.
      • Jannat al-Mu'alla: A significant cemetery in the holy city of Makkah, this is a revered site that houses the tombs of many of the relatives and other ancestors of the blessed Prophet Muhammed.
      • The Floating Mosque: An iconic site in Jeddah, this is also called the Masjid al-Rahmah, and is perched comfortably on a man-made platform on the Red Sea. It is a unique sight to enjoy when you book the best Umrah package 2026 from India.
      • Maktaba Makkah-al-Mukarramah: Located in Makkah, it is the birthplace of the holy Prophet Muhammed. A mountain earlier, it has now been remodelled into a celebrated national library.
      • Taif: The captivating city of Taif is famous for its verdant gardens, scented roses, abundant fruits, and several historical sites. Take a day trip to this scenic city once you complete your Umrah.
      • Masjid al-Qiblatayn: Located in the holy city of Madinah (or Medina), this beautiful mosque is where the Almighty commanded the blessed Prophet Muhammed to change the direction of prayer from Jerusalem to Qibla.
      • Hejaz Railway Museum: If you wish to see something unique on your trip when booking a luxury Umrah package, head to the Hejaz Railway Museum to learn a bit about the intriguing transportation history of the region.
      • Dar Al-Madinah Museum: Another fantastic museum, it is home to some incredible and rare artefacts and ancient collections, each depicting the remarkable heritage of the holy city of Madinah.

      Things to Do in Makkah and Madinah During Umrah Tour

      Booking a 30 days Umrah package from India is advisable, as it allows you to see more than just a handful of the popular sights in the holy cities of Makkah and Madinah. Here are some things to do when you book Umrah tour packages for your sacred pilgrimage:

      • Pray at Maqam Ibrahim: It is a consecrated site where the blessed Prophet Muhammed is believed to have stood while the Kaaba was under construction.
      • See the Prophet's Mosque: Located in Madinah, the sacred Al-Masjid an-Nabawi was built by the blessed Prophet Muhammed, and is his final resting place. Spending a while in prayer here will rejuvenate your soul.
      • Visit the holy sites of Hajj: Find time to head to sanctified places critical to the Hajj pilgrimage, such as the Mina, Arafat, Jabal al-Rahmah, and more.
      • Perform Tawaf: It is a pivotal ritual wherein pilgrims need to circle the Kaaba counterclockwise 7 times.
      • Perform Sa'ee: You must walk or run, if possible, between the important hills of Safa and Marwah 7 times. A crucial Umrah ritual, it commemorates the search for water by the blessed Prophet Ibrahim's wife Hajar.
      • Marvel at Quba Mosque: It is the first ever mosque built in the Islamic history! According to ancient accounts, the blessed Prophet himself laid the foundations for the mosque and lifted prayers here.
      • Drink Zamzam water: One drink from the miraculous and blessed Zamzam well is sure to quench your thirst. You will find this sacred spot inside the Masjid al-Haram.
      • Perform additional prayers: Pilgrims are advised to perform additional dhikr or Quran recitations for a deeper spiritual connection with the Almighty during their Umrah.
      • Journey to Mount Uhud: A must-visit site when booking the best Umrah package, Mount Uhud holds immense significance in Islamic history. It's where the second major battle occurred between Muslims and pagans.
      • Pay respects at Jannat al-Baqi: This pivotal and historically critical cemetery is home to the graves of several of the members of the blessed Prophet's family.
      • Visit the Al-Madinah Museum: The best way to get full value from your Umrah package from India besides visiting holy sites is to head to the Al-Madinah Museum. Here, you will discover the region's rich past through ancient manuscripts, artefacts, and interactive dioramas and displays.
      • Sightsee and shop for souvenirs: There are many affordable markets you can head to for a bit of shopping once you have completed your Umrah. The bustling local bazaars are great places to buy clothes for children, handmade items, jewellery, and lots more.
      • Savour the local cuisine: Trying some popular Saudi Arabian traditional food and drinks can elevate your trip through Umrah packages 2026. Popular items to sample include Arabic coffee (or Qawah), Shawarma, Mabshoor, Dates, and Kunafa.
