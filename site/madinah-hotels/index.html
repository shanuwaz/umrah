Madinah Hotels for Families and Groups

Hotel requirements can be different for every group. A couple may need one double or twin room, while a family may require several rooms or a combination of different room types.

If you are travelling as a larger group, tell us the number of people, rooms and preferred sharing arrangement when making your enquiry.

For example, if six people are travelling and you require three double rooms, include this information in your enquiry. This allows our team to check suitable room arrangements based on availability.

Families travelling with children or elderly pilgrims can also mention any specific requirements before requesting a quotation.

How to Choose a Hotel Near Masjid an-Nabawi

Before selecting your accommodation, consider the factors that matter most to your journey.

Distance from Masjid an-Nabawi: Check the stated distance and consider the actual route to the mosque. A shorter distance may be particularly useful for elderly pilgrims or families.

Hotel location: Hotels in the Central Area and surrounding parts of Madinah offer different levels of access to the Prophet's Mosque and nearby facilities.

Room configuration: Consider whether you need double, twin, triple or quad-sharing rooms and how many rooms your group requires.

Hotel category: Choose between different accommodation categories based on your preferred level of comfort and facilities.

Travel dates: Hotel availability and quotations can vary according to your check-in and check-out dates, so provide your exact travel dates when enquiring.
