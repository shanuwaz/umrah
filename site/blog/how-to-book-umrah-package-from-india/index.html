How to Book an Umrah Package from India: Complete Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

Build Your Umrah Package

Who Can Book an Umrah Package from India?

Any Muslim citizen or resident in India can book Umrah packages for families, couples, groups, or solo travel to Makkah. You must be at least 18 years old to book an Umrah package and travel independently. Minors (under 18) and infants and children (under 12) cannot travel for Umrah alone, and must be accompanied by legal guardians.

All Umrah pilgrims from India regardless of age must have separate visas and passports (with 6 months’ validity). In addition to valid Indian passports, you need a PAN card, proof of vaccination, and Nusuk-compliant hotel and flight booking confirmations for obtaining Umrah visas.

Ways to Book an Umrah Package from India

1. Book Through an Authorized Umrah Travel Agency (Recommended)

The most convenient, commonest way on how to book Umrah from India is through a registered, Saudi-approved Umrah agency like Al Mahad Travels. It eliminates the stress of logistical planning and financial worry with affordable Umrah deals for all budgets and travel styles, particularly helpful for first-time pilgrims.

The Umrah agency handles all arrangements, including hotels, flights, Umrah visas, local transfers (inter-city and hotel-airport), Ziyarats in Makkah and Madinah, meals, and tailored itineraries for your preferred trip duration.

2. Book Umrah Yourself Through the Nusuk Platform (DIY Booking)

Nusuk is the official online platform and mobile application designed by the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah to simplify pilgrimage experience. It allows Muslims globally to book accommodation, transfers, and permits for holy sites like the Noble Rawdah in Madinah. Pilgrims can download the Nusuk app for booking Umrah and integrate all their trip components.

Tech-savvy pilgrims and those who need flexible schedules, private logistics, and travel independence that fixed Umrah itineraries don't always offer, can attempt to self-book Umrah. However, Indians cannot directly book Umrah visas on the Nusuk platform, and need to go through a Saudi-ratified Umrah agency.

How to Choose the Right Umrah Travel Agency

●     Verify Saudi Ministry Authorization: Ensure the Indian Umrah agency is registered and directly approved by the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, with the license to organize Umrah trips through legitimate channels.

●     Check Reviews and Testimonials: Consult trusted online sources to verify the agency's track record of booking Umrah from India, and gauge pilgrim satisfaction with the travel agents.

●     Compare Package Inclusions: Umrah Package transparency is key, allowing you to know exactly what you're paying for. Check package inclusions carefully to make necessary adjustments before you finalize booking.

●     Verify Hotel Distance from Haram: Ask for the specific hotel distance from the Haram in meters, instead of settling for the star rating. If it isn't within walking distance from the Haram, find out whether shuttle service is provided.

●     Ask About Hidden Charges and Cancellation Policy: Enquire about hidden costs and review Umrah package cancellation and refund terms, as many bookings are non-refundable or have strict date-change penalties.

Step-by-Step Process to Book an Umrah Package from India

Step 1: Choose Your Umrah Travel Dates

Select the right Umrah travel dates for you by aligning your schedule with budget and travel preferences. Pick off-peak seasons (June to September and the months of Muharram and Safar immediately following Hajj) for more economical Umrah trips, shoulder months (March, April, and October) for moderate weather and fewer crowds, or peak seasons (Ramadan and December) for greater spiritual merit and blessings.

Step 2: Decide Your Budget and Package Type

Define your Umrah budget by the number of travel companions, hotel star rating and distance to Haram, trip duration, meal plan, and transfer type (private vs. shared). Standard economy Umrah packages cover all the basics and usually feature quad-sharing rooms in 3-star hotels a little further from the Haram. Deluxe/mid-range or premium/luxury Umrah packages offer 4 to 5-star hotels near the Haram with Kaaba-view rooms, private transfers, gourmet meals, and personalized itineraries.

Step 3: Shortlist and Contact Authorized Travel Agencies

Make a list of reliable Umrah agencies based on their IATA certification, official Saudi-approved credentials, and confirmed partnerships with Saudi-based Umrah operators. Solicit written, itemized quotes for Umrah packages with identical dates and services from each agency, with a detailed itinerary. Require exact package inclusions, from hotel names and star ratings to specific distance from the Haram, flight and ground transport information, visa services, and Nusuk-compliant BRN details.

Step 4: Compare Umrah Package Inclusions

Check hotels’ distance from the Haram in Makkah, and from Masjid an-Nabawi in Madinah, as long distances mean you must rely on shuttle services. Compare room sharing details, with cheaper packages often clubbing 4 to 6 people in a single room. See if you're flying with budget airlines, and whether the flights are direct or involve long layovers. Measure costs for transfers, meal plans, Ziyarat tours, and other package inclusions to know which one offers the better value.

Step 5: Submit Required Documents

A major part of learning how to book an Umrah package from India for family, group, or solo pilgrimage is knowing what documents you need to submit to your travel agency to obtain an Umrah visa. These include:

●     Indian passports valid for at least 6 months from your Umrah travel date

●     Scanned copies of the first and last page of your Indian passport

●     Passport-size, high-resolution color photographs taken recently against a plain white background

●     Confirmed return flight tickets or a multi-city Umrah travel itinerary mapping Saudi entry points

●     BRN-Verified hotel bookings with Nusuk approval

●     Transportation vouchers - intercity and hotel-airport

●     Valid Indian Pan Card copy

●     Mahram/relationship proof (if applicable), proving guardianship if women are traveling for Umrah alone without a Mahram

●     Additional documents if staying with family or friends in Saudi Arabia:

➢   Iqama copy of the Saudi resident you are staying with

➢   Saudi address of the Iqama holder

➢   Mobile number of the Iqama holder

Step 6: Confirm Your Booking and Make Payment

Most Umrah operators require 25-30% advance for booking, 50% midway, and the remaining amount before departure. Some Indian Umrah agencies or specialized services require 100% upfront payment for visa processing and BRN (Booking Reference Number) generation. Pay the applicable costs and lock in your Umrah package, confirming that you will receive a payment receipt upon transferring funds to the agency's official account.

Step 7: Receive Your Travel Documents

After successfully booking your Umrah package, you should receive core travel and verification documents in your email generally between 24 to 48 hours after processing. These include:

●     Umrah e-Visa PDF

●     Confirmed flight e-Tickets

●     Hotel vouchers (BRN)

●     Transportation/Transfer vouchers

●     Travel itinerary (day-wise trip details)

●     Local guide and Ziyarat tour operator details (if applicable)

●     Emergency contact details

Important Tip: Always carry physical, printed color copies of all of the above-listed documents, besides soft copies on your phone. This is because Saudi immigration authorities and airline check-in staff at Indian airports typically request hard copies.

What Documents Should You Receive After Booking?

●     Umrah e-Visa: The officially approved electronic visa document required to enter Saudi Arabia for your Umrah will contain your personal details, passport number, visa validity, and duration of stay.

●     Confirmed Flight E-Tickets: Your verified onward or round-trip flight tickets are shared usually with the PNR number, which you can independently confirm on the airline's official website.

●     BRN Hotel Confirmation: Once validated by the provider, a unique BRN is generated for your hotels in Makkah and Madinah, in compliance with mandates by the Saudi authorities.

●     Ground Transport Vouchers: Get documents confirming your pre-booked airport-hotel transfers, Tafweej permit, or inter-city transport, including drivers' names, vehicle type and license place, local contact number, and other ground operator details.

●     Travel Itinerary: Review your day-wise itinerary outlining a clear plan for the days you perform Umrah, including the Miqat location, scheduled Ziyarat tours and free time, transfer pick-up and drop-off points, and more.

●     Local Guide and Ziyarat Tour Operator Details: If applicable to the Umrah package, you will receive full details of the local guide/Mutawwif coordinating your trip, transfers, and Ziyarat excursions.

●     Agency Emergency Contacts: Keep our Umrah agent's contact number handy for assistance with trip cancellations, transfer rescheduling, last-minute itinerary changes, medical emergencies, etc.

Frequently Asked Questions

●     How early should I book an Umrah package from India?

Book Umrah packages from India at least 3 to 6 months in advance for peak seasons like Ramadan and December, locking in cheaper airfare and prime 5-star hotels near the Haram before prices surge. Book 2 to 3 months in advance for standard or off-peak Umrah months, securing flexible travel dates and excellent group Umrah packages.

●     Can I book Umrah without a travel agent?

Indians cannot directly apply for Umrah visa online from India, and must book a basic package with visa, accommodation, and transport through the Nusuk Umrah platform or a Saudi-approved Umrah travel provider. Al Mahad Travels offers bespoke Umrah packages from India catering to various budgets and travel preferences.

●     Is Nusuk mandatory for Umrah?

Yes, the Nusuk Umrah platform is mandatory for obtaining an official permit for performing your Umrah, as decreed by the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. International pilgrims are required to link their pre-booked hotel and transport BRN for their Umrah visa application.

●     How much does an Umrah package cost from India?

Umrah packages from India range from ₹65,000 to ₹1,50,000 per person for an economy or standard 7 to 15-day trip, usually including accommodation, flights, visas, Ziyarats, local transport, and meals. Mid-range to luxury Umrah packages cost around ₹90,000 to 3,50,00+ per person, depending on hotel star rating and proximity to the Haram, duration, luxury tier, flight costs, meal plan, and package inclusions.

●     Can families and senior citizens book Umrah packages?

Yes, families and senior citizens in India can easily book Umrah packages with Al Mahad Travels. Our dedicated staff and guides assist with Umrah rituals, passing immigration, finding family-friendly or wheelchair-accessible hotels, managing prayer schedules, transfer bookings, and customizing travel itineraries.

●     Are flights and hotels included in Umrah packages?

Al Mahad Travels provides land Umrah packages starting at just ₹49,000 to ₹52,999 per person for 7 to 14 nights, with basic 3-star accommodation usually on a quad-sharing basis. You can add flights to make it a full Umrah package, costing marginally more but eliminating logistical planning stress.
