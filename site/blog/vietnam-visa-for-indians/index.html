Vietnam Visa for Indians Complete Guide 2026: Eligibility, Types, Fees, Application Process, and Rules

The demand for a Vietnam visa for Indians has always been high, thanks to the Southeast Asian country's high affordability, direct flight connectivity from several major Indian cities, and a hassle-free e-Visa process. Visit Da Nang and Phu Quoc for beaches, Hanoi for a blend of history and culture, Hoi An for its enchanting lantern-lit ancient town, and the UNESCO-listed Ha Long Bay for breathtaking limestone karsts.

A Vietnam visa for Indians on arrival is available, but it comes with conditions. Indian citizens opting for a Vietnam visa on arrival (VoA) must obtain a letter of pre-approval online applied through a travel agency before boarding their flight to Vietnam (or Viet Nam). Alternatively, apply for a Vietnam eVisa before the trip through licensed, reliable visa travel agencies like Al Mahad Tours and Travels. What is a Vietnam Visa?

A Vietnam visa is an official travel document allowing foreign nationals to enter and travel within Vietnam for tourism, family visits, business, and other acceptable purposes. Citizens from some countries can get a visa on arrival in Vietnam, subject to relevant terms and conditions. Indians can apply for a single-entry or multiple-entry Vietnamese visa for their trip, following a few pivotal steps in the application process. The cost, entry type, validity, and processing times for Vietnam visas for Indians will change depending on the specific visa type.

Who Can Apply for a Vietnam Visa from India?

Is Vietnam visa-free for Indians is a common question among first-time tourists, and the answer is that it's not. All Indian passport holders are eligible to apply for a Vietnam visa for Indians, provided they qualify the specified criteria and have a clear purpose of travel. An electronic visa (e-Visa) is the commonest type for Vietnam that Indians can apply for, typically valid for up to 90 days and available in single-entry and multiple-entry variants. Vietnam visas are available for Indians for tourism, business, work, study, and transit.

Types of Vietnam Visas for Indian Citizens

There are three main options when applying for a Vietnam visa from India, the electronic visa (e-Visa), visa on arrival (VoA), and Embassy sticker visa. Indians can apply for visas to Vietnam for tourism, business, transit, work, investments, and other valid reasons. Refer to the following overview of the important types of Vietnam visas available to Indian citizens.

●Tourist eVisa: An electronic Vietnam tourist visa from India offers a 90-day validity and is available in both single-entry and multiple-entry variants. It is valid for stays of up to 90 days in Viet Nam.

●Visa on Arrival (VoA): For a visa on arrival, Indians need to get a pre-approved letter online through a licensed travel agency before boarding their flight to Vietnam. This is a mandatory step, without which a visa on arrival in Vietnam will not be issued.

●Business Visa: A Vietnam business visa from India can range in validity from 1 month to 1 year, and is designed for those attending meetings, trade fairs, conferences, and other professional events in Vietnam.

●Work/Employment Visa: Typically sponsored by a Vietnamese employer and valid for up to 2 years (or the duration of the work permit), a Vietnam work visa for Indians allows you to legally stay and work in the country.

●Student Visa (DH): A Vietnam student visa for Indians is usually valid for 1 year but can be extended, and has multiple entry options. It is for those who have enrolled in legitimate Vietnamese educational institutions/universities.

●Embassy Sticker Visa: A physical visa stamped directly into your Indian passport, it is obtained via the Vietnam Embassy or Consulate and typically supports tourism or business-related visits, with single or multiple entries.

●Investor Visa (DT): Categorized by investment amount (D1 to D4), a Vietnam investor visa is for Indians contributing capital investments in the country. It offers multiple-entry options valid for 1 to 10 years and allows for Temporary Residence Cards (TRC).

Documents Required for Vietnam Visa Application for Indian Citizens

In addition to recent color photographs and your original, valid Indian passport, you will need a specific set of documents when applying for a visa to Vietnam. Ensure the Vietnam visa for Indians photo size and other requirements are met unfailingly to mitigate the chances of rejection. Browse the below Vietnam visa for Indians documents required checklist to get started.

●Original Indian Passport: Your original Indian passport must be valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival in Vietnam. Additionally, there must be a minimum of 2 blank pages in the passport for stamps by the Vietnamese immigration authorities.

●Passport Data Page: Ensure you have scanned color copies of the front and back of your Indian passport, with all details clearly showing so the Vietnamese authorities can have a close look.

●Recent Color Photographs: Provide color copies of recently taken passport-size photographs against a plain white background and meet all criteria as specified by the Vietnamese officials.

●Flight Ticket Confirmation: Submit proof of return or onward travel via confirmed flight tickets along with your Vietnam visa application, showing intent to leave the country after your scheduled visit.

●Hotel Booking/Accommodation Proof: Have hotel booking confirmation or resort reservation as proof or where you'll be staying in Vietnam during your trip, and submit a detailed tour itinerary to make your visa application stronger.

●Visa Application Form: Complete your Vietnam visa application with the correct details, review everything, and duly sign the form before submitting it online, ensuring there are no errors.

●Vaccination Certificate: A Yellow Fever vaccination certificate is required only for Indians arriving from countries/regions at risk. It is advisable to stay updated on other routine vaccinations before the Vietnam trip.

●Travel Insurance: While it is not mandatory to have travel insurance to visit Vietnam, it is highly advisable to get a comprehensive policy covering sudden trip emergencies. This can also make your Vietnam visa application more legitimate, showing travel preparedness.

●Additional Documents: For business e-Visas, you'll need an official invitation letter from your Vietnamese sponsoring company, while minors (under 14) will need a copy of their birth certificate. For a Vietnam visa on arrival, you must have an official letter issued by the Vietnam immigration department before departure, obtained through a registered visa travel agency.

Applicants of student visas will need an official letter of admission from the respective Vietnamese university/institute they enrolled in and a pre-approval letter from the Vietnam immigration department, and so on.

Vietnam Visa Fees for Indians

The standard tourist Vietnam visa fees for Indians ranges approximately from ₹2,100 to ₹2,500 for single entry, and it can cost from ₹4,200 to ₹4,800 for multiple entry variants. The Vietnam sticker visa issued via the Embassy can cost anywhere from ₹3,000 and ₹11,300, depending on entry type and duration. The price of Vietnam business visas for Indians will also vary based on entry type and duration, starting from ₹2,100 and going up to ₹5,500.

Vietnam Visa for Indians Price Breakdown:

●Standard Tourist eVisa: ₹2,100 to ₹2,500 for single-entry, and ₹4,200 to ₹4,800 for multiple-entry

●Visa on Arrival (VoA): ₹4,100 to ₹5,600 for single-entry, and ₹6,200+ for multiple-entry

●Business Visa: ₹2,100 to ₹2,500 for single-entry, and ₹4,200 to ₹5,500 for multiple-entry

●Work/Employment Visa: ₹3,000 to ₹12,000

●Student Visa (DH): ₹2,100 for single-entry, and ₹4,200 for multiple-entry

●Embassy Sticker Visa: ₹3,000 to ₹4,500 for single-entry, and ₹5,500 to ₹8,400 for multiple-entry

●Investor Visa (DT): ₹2,100 to ₹13,000

Important Note:

●All the above-listed Vietnam visa cost estimates are approximates only, and the actual Vietnam visa price for Indians will change depending on the type, duration, season, and other significant factors.

●The Vietnam visa price from India does not include service charges and other miscellaneous expenses.

●Vietnam visa fees, including service costs, are non-refundable regardless of the outcome of the Vietnam visa application from India.

Vietnam Visa Processing Time

A standard Vietnam e-Visa for Indians takes approximately 3 to 5 working days, with express options of 1 to 2 working days or even a few hours for a higher cost. The Vietnam Embassy sticker visa for Indians can take much longer, around 10 to 15 working days, with expedited options of around 10 to 12 working days. The Vietnam student visa (DH) is usually processed between 5 to 10 working days, provided all papers (including a pre-approval letter from Vietnamese immigration authorities) are in order.

The Vietnam visa for Indians processing time can change subject to type, season, the volume of applications at the particular time, and documentation accuracy, among other pivotal criteria. Invariably, it's best to apply for a visa to Vietnam from India at least 1 to 2 weeks prior to your planned departure date, leaving ample room for unexpected delays and reapplication in case of a rejection.

Step-by-Step Online Vietnam Visa Application Process

Through the quick, easy following steps, learn how to apply for Vietnam visa from India any time during the year. Follow each step with care to ensure your visa for Vietnam is processed successfully the first time, without the need for reapplication.

Step 1: Prepare All Documents

●Valid Indian Passport: Your Indian passport must be original, and have at least 6 months' validity and a minimum of 2 blank pages for immigration stamps by Vietnamese authorities.

●Color Photos: Make sure the passport-size photographs you submit are clear, recent, taken against a plain white background, and meet all other criteria specified by the immigration department of Vietnam.

●Flight Itinerary: Submit return or onward flight ticket information as evidence of your intent to leave Vietnam after the scheduled trip.

●Accommodation Proof: Give resort reservation or hotel booking number and other accommodation proof, along with a detailed Vietnam tour itinerary for your stay.

●Vaccination Certificate: Provide a Yellow Fever vaccination certificate if arriving from a region/country at high risk, and stay updated on all other essential vaccination requirements.

●Travel Insurance: Although not compulsory, it is advisable to have a strong travel insurance policy for your Vietnam trip, protecting against unforeseen emergencies.

●Supporting Paperwork: Vietnam business visa applicants must provide a letter of invitation from their Vietnamese sponsoring company, student visa applicants must provide a letter of admission from the Vietnamese institute they enrolled in, work visa applicants must have a letter of employment/job appointment from their Vietnamese employer, minors (under 14) must have a copy of their birth certificate, and so on.

Step 2: Visit the Official Vietnam E-Visa Portal

●Log-In/Sign-Up: If you don't already have an account on the Vietnam eVisa portal, create one using a strong password, user name, and email.

●Find Your Visa Type: Select the Vietnam visa type applicable for your trip, and click on "apply" to begin the application process.

Step 3: Vietnam Visa Application Form

●Purpose of Travel: State your purpose of travel to Vietnam clearly, whether tourism, business, study, investment, or other.

●Personal Details: Ensure the information you enter into the Vietnam visa application form is correct, and that your name, address, and other details match that on the passport.

Step 4: Upload Documentation and Form

●Clear Copies: The photos and documents you scan and upload for Vietnam visa must be clear and blur-free, else the application can be rejected.

●Final Check: Read through the visa application form one last time and check other essential documents before uploading them on the Vietnam e-Visa portal.

Step 5: Pay Vietnam Visa Fee and Submit Application

●Visa Cost: Depending on the visa type, pay the applicable Vietnam visa fee for Indians through the payment options given on the page.

●Service Charge: If you're applying for a Vietnam visa through an agency, you must pay a small service cost for the application, at the time the agency specifies you to do so.

●Accurate Card Details: Enter correct card details when paying for your Vietnam visa, or you will need to begin the entire process all over again.

Step 6: Track Status and Await Approval

●Processing Time: A standard Vietnam tourist eVisa is processed within 3 to 3 working days, with the express option of 1 to 2 working days or even a couple of hours.

●Tracking/Reference Number: Use the reference number you get after submitting the application for a Vietnam visa to track the status every now and again.

●Visa Agency: When you apply through an agency, your visa agent can give you real-time updates on the status of your Vietnam visa application, so you know when to expect it.

Step 7: Receive Vietnam eVisa and Download Copy

●Visa via Email: After approval, you will receive your Vietnam eVisa in a PDF format via email, linked to your passport number. It will include all your personal information, visa type, validity period, passport number, duration of stay, and so on.

●Download and Print Copy: You need to download and print a copy (or two, as a backup) of your Vietnam eVisa before travel, to present it at the Vietnamese immigration check point upon arrival.
