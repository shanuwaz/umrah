Tanzania Visa for Indians Complete Guide 2026: Eligibility, Types, Fees, Application Process, and Rules

Scores of tourists with a wide variety of travel preferences book a Tanzania visa for Indians each year, hoping to explore exotic wildlife in Serengeti and Ngorongoro, the ethereal beaches of Zanzibar, and the iconic dormant volcano Mount Kilimanjaro. Additionally, with a good number of locals speaking English and a navigable transport system, travel to Tanzania has become more hassle-free and enjoyable for Indian tourists in recent years.

While a Tanzania visa on arrival is available for Indians, it is prudent to secure it well before the trip to prevent delays or potential rejections. What is a Tanzania Visa?

A Tanzania visa is a necessary official travel authorization permitting foreign nations to enter into and explore Tanzania for a specified period of time. They can avail of a visa to visit Tanzania for various purposes, including tourism and sightseeing, business, study, work, and transit. Please note that final entry into Tanzania depends on immigration authorities.

Depending on the type, the Tanzania visa can be single or multiple-entry, allowing stays for up to a specified period of time within the validity period. The documents required to apply for a visa to Tanzania from India must be kept ready beforehand, and there may be additional paperwork necessary for particular types.

Who Can Apply for a Tanzania Visa from India?

All Indian passport holders with a clear purpose of visit and meeting specified criteria can apply for a visa to travel to Tanzania. This can be for tourism, work, study, business, or transit reasons. The process of visa application is simple and can be done online at the official Tanzania e-Visa portal. A Visa on Arrival Tanzania for Indian citizens is possible, but there may be higher chances of rejections or delays at the checkpoint, as final authority rests with the Tanzanian immigration authorities. Types of Tanzania Visas for Indian Citizens

Officially called the United Republic of Tanzania, this beautiful East African country offers several different types of visas for Indians to visit, based on the purpose of travel and needs. Subsequently, the processing time and eligibility requirements may vary from one visa type to another.

The following is a quick brief of the different types of Tanzania visa Indian nationals can apply for. They include:

● Tourist Visa/e-Visa: A Tanzania tourist visa from India is useful and necessary for short visits to see family or friends, sightseeing, and general tourism. It is available as single-entry and multiple-entry, and is usually valid for up to 90 days.

● Visa on Arrival (VoA): Indians can get a visa on arrival in Tanzania usually for tourism, transit, and often for short business trips. There are specific rules for each type, and thereby different entry types and validity periods.

● Student Visa: You will need a Tanzania student visa from India if you have enrolled in a Tanzanian university for a study course. The visa is typically valid for the duration of the academic program, and in certain cases you must apply for a Student Pass (residence permit).

● Business Visa: Usually single-entry and valid for up to 90 days, a Tanzania business visa from India allows you to attend meetings, trade fairs, and conferences, and explore partnerships with local Tanzanian businesses, establish contacts, and discover other viable professional ventures.

● Work Visa: A Tanzania work visa from India can offer multi-year validity and generally requires employer application. It is for Indians employed by a Tanzanian company and requires several different documents to be submitted along with the visa application form.

● Multiple Entry Visa: This type of Tanzania visa is suitable for Indians with frequent travel needs to the country, such as for business, investments, or meetings. It can be valid for up to 1 year, allowing multiple entries into Tanzania with maximum stays of up to 90 days per visit.

● Investor Visa: The Tanzania investor visa for Indians is divided into various types. For instance, those investing significant amounts into a business in Tanzania must apply for a “Class A Residence Permit”, offering multi-year validity. Others can opt for a multiple-entry business visa or a short-term e-Visa, and so on.

● Volunteer Visa: Commonly a single-entry visa valid for around 90 days, a volunteer visa for Tanzania from India is for those participating in social or NGO programs, and often requires specific approval. It also has a multiple-entry variant with different eligibility and validity criteria.

● Transit Visa: The single-entry transit visa to Tanzania is for Indians traveling through the country to an onward destination. It is generally valid for 7 days, requires proof of onward travel (air ticket), and must be applied for online beforehand.

● Others: Indians can also apply for medical visa, missionary visa, medical attendant visa, journalist visa, and other types of visa to Tanzania depending on the purpose of visit and subject to approval.

Documents Required for Tanzania visa Application for Indian Citizens

Whether applying online or making arrangements for a Tanzania visa on arrival documents, a valid Indian passport is a must for the application. There are other essential papers to submit, and some visa types require additional supporting documents or certificates.

The following is a common checklist for most types of visas for Tanzania from India. They include:

●Valid Indian Passport:

Your passport must be authentic, valid for a minimum of 6 months from the date of arrival in Tanzania, and have at least 2 blank pages for stamps by the Tanzanian immigration authorities.

●Yellow Fever Vaccination Certificate:

Tanzania requires a Yellow Fever vaccination certificate from travelers from India and several other countries, issued at least 10 days before travel.

●Passport-Size Color Photos:

You need 2 to 3 passport-sized color photographs taken not over 3 months ago against a blur-free white background. The photos must meet all other criteria listed by the Tanzanian officials.

●Flight Itinerary:

Provide evidence of your intention to leave Tanzania after your visit by submitting copies of your return or onward flight tickets along with the visa application form.

●Hotel/Accommodation Proof:

Give details of your hotel room booking confirmation, or provide a letter of invitation from your Tanzanian resident friend or family members if staying with them.

●Funds Statement:

Get copies of your most recent bank statements, dating a minimum of 3 to 6 months back, showing sufficient finances for your trip to and stay in Tanzania.

●Travel Insurance:

Although not a necessity, getting travel insurance can increase chances of approval for a Tanzania visa from India, as it proves you are well prepared for the journey.

●Purpose-Specific Documents:

For business visa, offer a letter of invitation on a company letterhead from a legitimate Tanzanian company. For student visa, submit a copy of your letter of admission at the Tanzanian university you enrolled in. For minors, a birth certificate is mandatory, and the like.

The Tanzania visa fees for Indians for a single-entry tourist visa is approximately ₹4,200 onward, while a multiple-entry visa valid for up to 1 year can range from ₹8,300 to ₹8,500 INR. A single-entry business visa for Tanzania is priced at approximately ₹19,000 or more, and a Tanzania student visa can range from ₹20,000 to ₹21,000, or more.

Tanzania Visa Processing Time

An online Tanzania visa for Indians is usually processed from 2 to 7 business days, whereas applications through visa agents or the High Commission can take around 5 to 10 business days. Typically, the standard processing time for a tourist e-Visa to Tanzania from India is approximately 10 to 12 business days, although there are express options for a faster service. The exact time for processing your visa to Tanzania will depend on the visa type, volume of applications (more in the high travel season and vice versa), documentation accuracy, and other applicable factors. Subsequently, it is best to apply for the visa well in advance of travel, leaving room for reapplication and potential delays.

How to Apply for a Tanzania Visa Online from India?

The Tanzania visa application for Indians process is fairly simple. First, visit the official Tanzania immigration website and log in by creating an account. Select the visa type and fill the online Tanzania visa application form with the required details correctly. Upload clear scanned copies of the essential documents and pay the fees, according to the latest applicable Tanzania visa for Indians price. You may need to visit a consulate for biometric verification in some instances, so apply well in advance.

Once you have submitted your visa application to Tanzania from India, you must wait out the stipulated processing period, which is usually between 10 and 12 days. After approval, you will receive your Tanzania e-Visa via email, and it is recommended to carry a printed hard copy of this during travel.

Alternatively, you can apply for a visa to Tanzania via a registered and experienced travel agency who can expedite the process, handle all formalities (documentation, form submission, tracking status, etc.), and help get your visa approved quickly.

Step-by-Step Online Tanzania Visa Application Process

The following is a quick and easy way to learn how to apply for a Tanzania visa from India. Browse each step carefully to know what the process entails, ensuring you obtain your visa to travel to Tanzania successfully.

Step 1: Prepare All Documents

● Indian Passport: Verify that your passport is valid for a period of 6 months from the date of entry into Tanzania, and has a minimum of 2 blank pages for immigration stamps.

● Yellow Fever Vaccination Certificate: Indian travelers need to provide a Yellow Fever vaccination certificate, issued at least 10 days prior to the date of travel to Tanzania.

● Passport-Sized Color Photos: The photographs must be clear and blur-free, taken against a plain white background and with all your facial features showing.

● Flight Details: Submit copies of your flight itinerary, including return or onward tickets to prove your intent of leaving Tanzania after the visit.

● Proof of Accommodation: Provide hotel booking confirmation or a letter of invitation to visit from your Tanzanian host (friends or family), if you will be staying with them during the visit.

● Bank Statement: Print the last 3 to 6 months of your bank account statements, showing you have adequate money to travel to and stay in Tanzania.

● Travel Insurance: While it isn't mandatory, travel insurance is highly recommended and a great way to show preparedness for your trip to Tanzania. It can help obtain your Tanzania visa more quickly.

● Additional Supporting Papers: Business visa applicants must submit a letter of invitation from a Tanzanian company, while students must offer a letter of admission from the Tanzanian university they enrolled in. Minors require a birth certificate, while work visa applicants must provide a letter of employment appointment from their Tanzanian employer, and so forth.

Step 2: Log into the Official Tanzania Immigration Portal

● Create Account: Register on the e-Visa website with a valid email address and a strong password to start your visa application journey.

● Select Visa Type: Choose the particular type of visa you need for your trip to Tanzania, depending on the purpose of visit.

Step 3: Fill the Tanzania Visa Application Form

● Personal Information: Provide your full name, as per passport, date of birth, reason for travel, and other required information.

● Accurate Travel Dates: Make sure the travel dates you enter are correct, else the application will not be processed successfully.

● Verify Details: Go through all the filled in fields on the form and rectify any errors you may find.

Step 4: Upload Scanned Copies of the Documents

● Attach Files: All scans of the paperwork you update on the website to support your visa application should be clear.

● Re-Check Papers: Take a final look at everything you upload on the portal to ensure you haven't missed anything important.

Step 5: Pay Tanzania Visa Fees and Submit Application

● Tanzania Visa Fee: At the payment page, use a suitable mode of payment to pay the Tanzania visa fee, with the amount changing based on the visa type.

● Service Charge: When applying through a visa specialist or travel agency, you must pay a small service charge using the mode of payment specified by them.

● Card Information: Be careful when you input your debit or credit card details, as any mistake will cause you to start the payment process all over again.

Step 6: Track Visa Status and Receive Approval

● User ID or Reference Number: After submitting your visa application to Tanzania, you can track progress/status using your login credentials or the reference number provided after the application submission.

● Via Visa Agent: Your visa agency can keep a watch on your application on your behalf, keeping you updated on the current status every few days.

● Receive e-Visa Via Email: Your visa to Tanzania will be approved after the specified processing time of about 3 to 12 days. You will then receive your Tanzania e-Via via email.

● Print e-Visa: It is highly recommended that Indian travelers take a print out of their Tanzania tourist e-Visa before travel, as it makes travel smoother.
